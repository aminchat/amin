Capital Management · مدیریت سرمایه

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Summary: Capital Management (مدیریت سرمایه) is a personal finance web app. Your data is stored in your browser. An optional Google connection synchronizes it with your own Google Drive. The optional smart scanning feature sends the image you select to Google's Gemini service for processing.

1. Scope and contact

This policy applies to the Capital Management web app at aminchat.github.io/amin, with its source repository at aminchat/amin. The app's publisher and support contact can be reached at amin20051@gmail.com for privacy questions and requests. This app is not an official Google product.

2. Information the app processes

  • Financial information you enter: Accounts, transactions, amounts, budgets, assets, debts, notes, and related settings are processed to provide the app's features.
  • Google sign-in information: If you sign in, your name, email address, and profile picture are received from Google's sign-in response and stored in your browser to display your account and sign-in status. The developer does not need to add your email address in advance; this is different from the app itself receiving your email address.
  • Connection credentials: A temporary Google access token and its expiration time are kept in browser storage to communicate with Drive. The app does not receive your Google account password.
  • Optional scanning: The receipt, invoice, or transaction-sheet image you select is sent to Gemini. For transaction-sheet scanning, existing account names are also sent to help identify the account. The extracted output is processed for use in your financial records.
  • Gemini API key: If you enter your own API key, it is stored in your browser and used to make requests directly to Google's service.

Biometric unlock and reminders: If you enable fingerprint/face unlock, the browser creates a passkey-style credential on your device (WebAuthn). Your biometric data never leaves the device and is never sent to the developer or Google; the app only receives a yes/no result plus a small random secret stored in your browser that helps unlock local data. If you enable reminders for debts and installments, the browser asks for notification permission; notifications are generated locally and no server is involved.

3. Storage and synchronization

The current app is a static web app without a dedicated backend for storing user data. App data and settings are stored in the browser, including localStorage. A Service Worker may also cache app files for offline use.

When you connect to Google, the app's financial data is saved and synchronized in a file named capital-app-data.json in your Google Drive account. The developer does not receive a copy of this information through a central user database. This does not cover information you voluntarily email to support.

If the app's encryption feature is enabled, an encrypted version of the data is used for synchronization. Otherwise, synchronized data may be readable JSON. Do not assume that all browser information, including account details, connection tokens, and the API key, is covered by the encryption used for financial data.

Export and import: Settings → “Backup” lets you download all app data as a JSON file and restore it later. Exported files are unencrypted unless you protect them yourself; store them safely.

4. Google Drive access and use of Google data

The app requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. This is used to create, read, or update app-related files and files you make available to the app; it does not grant general access to all files in your Drive.

Data received from Google services is used only for the app's user-facing features, such as displaying your account, storage, and synchronization. It is not used to sell information, serve targeted advertising, or assess creditworthiness. The developer does not use this data to train general-purpose AI models.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Optional submission of information to Gemini is explained separately below.

5. Third-party services and smart scanning

  • Google: Sign-in, Drive, and, if used, Gemini are provided by Google and are subject to the relevant service terms and policies. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Gemini API: Only when you use scanning, your selected image and processing instructions, which may include your account names, are sent directly from your browser to Google. Scanning is not required for manual data entry. Redact unnecessary sensitive information before sending an image. Google's retention, review, and use of inputs and outputs depend on the service type, account, and current terms. See the Gemini API Terms. Sending information to Gemini does not mean that processing is local-only or that absolute confidentiality is guaranteed.
  • Hosting and external resources: GitHub Pages serves the app's files. The current version uses Bunny Fonts for fonts and a Google script for sign-in. When these resources load, their providers may receive ordinary technical request information, such as your IP address and browser details, under their own policies. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and Bunny Privacy Policy.

6. Retention, deletion, and revoking access

  1. To stop the connection, use the app's “Sign out of Google account” option. Signing out alone does not delete local financial data or the Drive file, and it does not replace revoking permission in your Google account.
  2. To revoke permission, find the app in your Google Account connections and remove its access.
  3. To delete the cloud copy, delete capital-app-data.json from Drive. For permanent deletion, empty the Drive trash using Google's available controls. Disconnect synchronization first to prevent the file from being recreated.
  4. To delete local data, clear the site's data in browser settings on every device you have used. On GitHub Pages, doing so may also delete data for other apps on the same aminchat.github.io origin. Clearing browser data does not delete the Drive copy.
  5. To remove the Gemini key from your browser, use “Delete key” in scanning settings. This does not invalidate the key at Google; use Google's key management tools to revoke it.

Local data and the Drive file may remain until deleted by you, your browser, or the relevant service. Backups and exports you have created must be deleted separately. Third-party retention is governed by the relevant provider's policies. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to address requests and meet applicable requirements; contact us to request its deletion.

7. Security and your choices

Use a trusted device, a device lock, and a strong password. Hiding numbers in the interface is not encryption. Malicious extensions, other people's access to your browser, or web vulnerabilities may expose stored data and keys; no storage method is completely secure. If you use locking and encryption, keep your recovery information safe. The developer may be unable to recover locked data.

You can use local features without connecting Google and enter information manually without Gemini. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you directly shared with support, email us. Do not send passwords, tokens, API keys, or complete financial files to support.

8. Policy changes

If the app's functionality or related services change, this policy will be updated and its date revised. Material changes will be announced on this page and, where possible, within the app. Updating this text does not replace obtaining new consent or permission where required.
